## Requirements reconciliation

- The two source briefs vary on bank naming and older corpus paths. The app uses **ABC Knowledge**, and `CORPUS_DIR` defaults to this project's `KM_Data`; docs do not imply real institutional endorsement.
- The later engineering brief permits local embeddings and single-service deployment, while the initial brief requests OpenRouter. Both are supported as explicit modes; the requested external provider remains OpenRouter.
- The briefs ask for two tasks in some places and four in another. The demo guide plans four tasks, including the two principal multi-step paths, without claiming a deployed recording exists.
- No paid database is required to run the local demo. Infrastructure mode deliberately requires PostgreSQL, OpenSearch and S3 and should not be presented as the lightweight default.
- The source requirement includes an exposed credential. Rotate/revoke it and remove it from shared artifacts/history where permitted. It is not reproduced here.

## Target production controls (not implemented claims)

Separate application, ingestion and MCP services; private network boundaries; bank identity provider; deny-by-default object permissions and model egress policy; KMS encryption and secrets manager; malware scanning/parser isolation; durable queue with idempotency/dead-letter handling; immutable external audit sink; OpenSearch replicas and capacity planning; PostgreSQL backup and failover; licence enforcement; independently signed approvals; OCR/claim-entailment evaluation; monitoring and disaster recovery drills. Prove these before handling real banking data or making availability/scale assurances.
