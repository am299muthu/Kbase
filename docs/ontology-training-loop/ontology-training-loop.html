KBase Ontology Training Loop

Planned design. People review every link, and their decisions teach a small model to do more of the routine work.

3. One Link, Start to Finish (made-up example)

  1. Approved document says“A dormant account is an account with no customer activity for 12 months.”
  2. Graph Extraction proposesTwo entities, “Account Policy” and “Dormant account”, and a link between them.
  3. DeBERTa Classifier picks“Dormant account” is a Term. The link is “defines”. Score 0.94, so no second opinion is needed.
  4. Reviewer decidesApprove. The link joins the knowledge graph and can be used in answers.
  5. Training Labels keepThe sentence, the guess “defines” (silver) and the decision “approved” (gold).
