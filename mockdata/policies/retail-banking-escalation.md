# SYNTHETIC retail escalation supplement

This is SYNTHETIC demonstration material, not bank policy or legal advice.

## Escalation evidence packet
For high-risk customer due diligence escalation, include the identity verification outcome and the reason the customer is classified high-risk. The escalation evidence packet must reference the retained customer due diligence record. This supplement does not replace the retail banking policy's 1 business day Compliance escalation deadline or 7-year retention requirement.
